# Independent lesson-acceptance study

Read [README.md](README.md) and [sources/README.md](sources/README.md). This project
owns workload discovery, methods, protocols, execution, diagnosis and publication.
Construct-2 owns theory and synthesis. In an assigned session, proceed autonomously
through bounded development and fresh experiments; preparation launches no runs.

- Investigate the evidence worth buying before accepting a reusable lesson.
  Evaluate later complete work against competent cheap checks and raw-experience
  reuse. Preserve original LA1–LA3 and unsuccessful attempts.
- Keep candidate generation, acceptance, scope revision and later reading
  distinguishable. Preserve source observations and acquired code in every arm.
  A successful source episode or declared authority flag does not prove a lesson.
- Choose workload and models through feasibility checks. Real public artifacts
  and motivated constructed episodes are both allowed with explicit provenance.
  Diagnose failed execution/acquisition purposefully; do not manufacture headroom
  by withholding ordinary tools, evidence or a cheap complete solution.
- Separate source feedback, probe evidence, development and fresh evaluation.
  Final truth must not leak into model context or checking tools. Candidate
  selection on final outcomes requires a new confirmation, preserving the old run.
- Record complete outcomes, unchanged responsibilities, actual evidence exposure
  and all known acquisition/checking/use/repair costs. Separate shared collection,
  experimental search and deployment work, keeping unknown costs and native units.
- Keep environments, code, evidence and owned instrument changes here. Inspect
  sibling publications read-only; pin provenance and preserve licenses when
  reusing artifacts. Do not edit Construct-2, other studies or the derivative's
  active checkout. `.deps/construct-runtime` is this study's owned pinned copy.
- Choose the smallest useful comparison. No general framework, learned router,
  adapter growth or skill representation is required. Neural learning is available
  when it resolves the question; verify actual acquisition and state isolation.
- Continue focused public-method discovery as findings sharpen the uncertainty.
  Record exact paper versions and distinguish inspected methods, author reports,
  local evidence and leads. Use the arXiv query API for metadata, cache responses,
  use a descriptive User-Agent, and keep API/OAI requests on one connection at
  least three seconds apart across controlled clients; honor Retry-After.

## Resources and autonomy

- Dedicated Mac Studio M1, 64 GB unified memory, with preferred serving through
  Docker Model Runner over Tailscale:
  `https://mac-studio-7hr7.taile71f88.ts.net/engines/v1/chat/completions`.
  Check current availability. Serving access does not establish gradients or
  adapter loading; the first comparison does not require either.
- The pinned runtime demonstrates native MLX inference/training on Apple silicon.
  Check available local memory and model/task competence before substantial runs.
  The earlier 4B/1.5B choices are evidence, not mandatory model sizes.
- Local open-weight models through `docker model`; OpenAI models through `codex`;
  SpaceXAI models through `agent`. Disclose teacher/comparison work and its costs.
  Preparation reserves no hardware and establishes no extra spending allowance.
- Coordinate heavy work and timing on shared machines. Start with a small pilot,
  set an explicit local budget before expansion, and raise genuinely missing
  access or resources beyond existing authorization. Routine scientific choices
  do not require returning to root for approval.
- Use `uv` for Python and Docker/Compose where useful. Keep credentials, caches,
  environments and base weights out of Git. Preserve manifests, evidence and
  small artifacts necessary to evaluate claims. Keep tool side effects local.

Completion of a publication does not revoke the commission. Assess and pursue a
promising bounded next step within the question when it can explain a limitation.
Stand down on explanatory progress, a demonstrated limit or a concrete resource
constraint; explain the reason briefly. A positive treatment effect is unnecessary.
Keep README current with a concise abstract and reproduction links. Shared process
should stay proportional to the research.
